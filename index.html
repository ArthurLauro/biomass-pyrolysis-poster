Development of a Digital Twin Framework for a Biomass Pyrolysis Reactor Applied to Biochar Production

Arthur Moreira Lauro · Pedro Paulo de Oliveira Rodrigues
University of Brasília (UnB), Brasília, Brazil

Introduction

  • Climate change mitigation and biomass-based energy conversion are urgent global priorities.
  • Brazil generated approximately 779.6 million tons of agricultural residues in 2021.
  • Forestry activities produced around 4.95 million tons of residues in 2019.
  • Biochar offers potential for soil quality improvement and long-term carbon storage.
  • Efficient pyrolysis requires reliable real-time process monitoring and decision support.

Methodology

  • Laboratory-scale fixed-bed pyrolysis reactor instrumented with Arduino-based control.
  • Digital twin framework integrated real-time data acquisition and process state tracking.
  • Eucalyptus grandis wood pellets were used as feedstock.
  • Continuous temperature sensing and mass monitoring were implemented.
  • Node-RED dashboard supported real-time visualization and supervisory analysis.
  • Thermochemical quality evaluated through Van Krevelen metrics (H/C and O/C ratios).

Results

  • Thermogravimetric behavior was compared at 300°C and 310°C.
  • Distinct mass-loss profiles and temperature evolution curves were observed between treatments.
  • Van Krevelen analysis indicated H/C reduction from 1.058 → 0.990.
  • O/C ratio decreased from 0.507 → 0.464.
  • Solid yield dropped from 45.2 wt% to 37.4 wt%, indicating stronger devolatilization.

Conclusions

  • The digital twin successfully captured key biomass pyrolysis stages.
  • The platform reached a Level III digital twin capability for real-time monitoring.
  • The system proved feasible for monitoring thermochemical conversion processes.
  • Acquired data supports preliminary biochar quality assessment.
  • Future work includes broader experimental campaigns and predictive model integration.
Keywords: Biomass Pyrolysis · Digital Twin · Biochar · Real-Time Monitoring · Thermochemical Conversion
